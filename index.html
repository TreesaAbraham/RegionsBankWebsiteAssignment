Who We Are

Full-service banking for Columbia.

Regions Bank's Columbia branch at 1010 Gervais Street is a full-service bank for individuals, families, small businesses, and commercial customers across Columbia and Richland County. We help people with everything from opening a first checking account to financing a home or growing a business, all backed by a regional bank that serves customers in 15 states.
